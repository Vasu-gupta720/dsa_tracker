## Suggested Order of Attack
1. Foundations: Activity Selection, Merge Intervals, Non-overlapping Intervals
2. Arrays: Sorting-based greedy, Partition Labels, Largest Number
3. Jump Game family: LC 55, LC 45 (both mandatory for every interview)
4. Strings: Remove K Digits, Reorganize String, Task Scheduler
5. Heap-based greedy: Top-K problems, IPO, Furthest Building
6. Candy and distribution: Gas Station, Candy, Queue Reconstruction
7. Transactions: Stock II, Job Scheduling, Video Stitching
8. Monotonic Stack: Daily Temperatures, Largest Rectangle, Trapping Rain Water
9. Constructive: Wiggle Subsequence, Create Maximum Number
10. Advanced CP: Only after all above are solid

## Notes
- Greedy problems uniquely require proving your approach is optimal — the "greedy stays ahead" argument. Practice justifying why a greedy choice is globally optimal.
- Many greedy problems look like DP until you realize a local optimal choice always leads to a global optimum — identifying this is the hardest skill.
- For interval greedy: always clarify whether intervals are open or closed and whether ties should be broken by start or end time.
- GFG links: if any slug 404s, search the title on geeksforgeeks.org/problems directly.
- For advanced Codeforces greedy: use the tag filter https://codeforces.com/problemset?tags=greedy — sort by rating 1200-2000 and solve in order.
